Built around the appointment

The booking page and the diary share the same rules.

Instead of maintaining a public form separately from your working calendar, Grab&Book uses the services, staff and availability you configure to decide what customers can actually book.

01

Services & booking rules

Set price, duration, buffers, booking questions, payment requirements and promotional offers at service level.

02

Availability that stays honest

Publish working blocks, recurring availability and time off so customers only see slots that fit your rules.

03

Team scheduling

Connect services to eligible staff, give team members their own availability and support flexible team booking.

04

Payments & deposits

Take full payment, fixed or percentage deposits, or pay-later bookings depending on the plan.

05

Confirmations & reminders

Send booking confirmations and reminders, with customer links for rescheduling and cancellation.

06

Calendar connection

Keep Grab&Book availability and external commitments aligned through Google Calendar integration.
