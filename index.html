Questions small businesses ask

What is Grab&Book and where does it fit?

Open a question if you want more detail.

What is Grab&Book?

Grab&Book is booking software for service businesses that connects online appointments with customer CRM and growth analytics, so booking activity does not sit separately from customer and marketing data.

Who is Grab&Book for?

It is designed for independent and growing appointment-led service businesses, from solo owners taking their first bookings to small teams that need a clearer system for customers, scheduling and growth.

Does Grab&Book include a customer CRM?

Yes. The customer CRM brings booking history, customer value, notes, consent and retention signals together around the same customer record.

Can Grab&Book show which marketing creates bookings?

The Growth analytics area is designed to connect available acquisition data with first-party booking outcomes and customer value. Unmatched or unavailable attribution stays unassigned rather than being guessed.

How payment fees work
Grab&Book

Free takes a 2% platform fee from each paid booking. Pro reduces the Grab&Book platform fee to 0%.

Stripe

Payment-processing fees apply separately on both plans. Stripe currently lists standard UK card payments at 1.5% + 20p; other card types and payment methods can cost more.

The customer pays the service price you set. These fees are deducted from the business proceeds; Grab&Book does not add a separate customer surcharge at checkout. Stripe rates can change.
