Customer intelligence

A CRM built from actual appointment behaviour.

The useful part is not just storing names and email addresses. Grab&Book uses booking history to help organise customers around value, frequency and whether they appear to be drifting away.

Keep bookings, history, notes, contact information, tags and marketing consent around the same customer.

Retention

Do more than count customers. Understand their rhythm.
