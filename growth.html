Why this matters

A channel can look weaker on first-booking conversion but create customers who return more often. Growth is designed to make that trade-off visible.

Deeper than acquisition

A cheap booking is not always a valuable customer.

Growth is structured to help you compare channels on what happens after acquisition as well as before it. That means new-customer volume can sit alongside repeat behaviour and customer value.
